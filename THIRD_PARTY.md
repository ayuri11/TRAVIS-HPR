# Third-party material

`01_neutronics/Define_OpenMC.py` is adapted from the KRUSTY OpenMC/Nektar++ coupling model of Xiao et al. (2019),
https://github.com/bearsan/Heat-Pipe-Reactor. Materials, geometry, tallies and settings were rewritten for TRAVIS. Please refer to the
upstream repository for its terms of use.

The heat-pipe wall relations follow Price et al. (2023); the helium-gap correction follows Genoni et al.
OpenMC and OpenFOAM are used as external tools and are not redistributed here.
